# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-14

First public release, following local development and playtesting.

- Purchasable church arcade cabinet, priced at 10 stamps by default.
- Backpack and church-chest ingredient scanning.
- Collapsed recipe groups, native-style rows and lazy expansion.
- Craftable default list, optional uncraftable recipes, search and scrolling.
- Healing/effect/item filters and healing/quantity/ingredient sorts.
- Learned-recipes-only setting, cream/yellow text, and center-carpet orientation.
- Stable per-save purchase unlock and repeated interaction.
- Public GPL-3.0 source, build instructions and 30 automated checks.
